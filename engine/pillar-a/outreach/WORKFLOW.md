# AI-Powered Client Outreach Workflow

## Tool Split: Claude vs Gemini

| Task | Tool | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Lead discovery (broad search) | **Gemini web UI** | Built-in Google Search, good at scanning many results fast |
| Company research + qualification | **Claude API** (`research_lead.py`) | Structured JSON output, follows ICP scoring consistently |
| Email generation | **Claude API** (`research_lead.py`) | Matches our templates exactly, validates against OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md |
| Batch processing a CSV | **Claude API** (`batch_pipeline.py`) | Unattended, runs overnight on a list |
| Final review before send | **You** | AI accuracy is not 100% — always read before sending |

---

## End-to-End Pipeline

```
Step 1 — FIND       Gemini web → list of 20-50 qualified company names
   ↓
Step 2 — RESEARCH   research_lead.py → JSON + ICP score per company
   ↓
Step 3 — FILTER     Discard score < 7. Keep everything ≥ 7.
   ↓
Step 4 — REVIEW     Read each _research.json — correct any hallucinations
   ↓
Step 5 — EMAILS     Already generated by research_lead.py → in _emails.md
   ↓
Step 6 — PERSONALISE Edit the first sentence of each email (2 min per email)
   ↓
Step 7 — SEND       Paste into Gmail / Instantly.ai / Lemlist
   ↓
Step 8 — LOG        Add to CRM_TRACKER.md → stage = "Contacted"
```

---

## Step 1 — Lead Discovery with Gemini

Open [Gemini Advanced](https://gemini.google.com) and paste the prompt from:
```
pillar-a/outreach/prompts/gemini_lead_discovery.md
```

Customise: vertical, funding stage, region, number of companies.

Gemini will search Google and output a table of companies with contacts.

**Export the table:** copy → paste into `pillar-a/outreach/leads.csv`
(use the column format in `leads_template.csv`)

---

## Step 2 — Research a Single Company (CLI)

```bash
# One company, provide the website URL
python pillar-a/outreach/scripts/research_lead.py \
  "Acme Corp" \
  --url https://acme.com \
  --output pillar-a/outreach/output/

# Output:
#   output/acme-corp_research.json   — full research + ICP score
#   output/acme-corp_emails.md       — 3 email variants, ready to edit
```

**Requires:** `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set in environment, plus:
```bash
pip install anthropic duckduckgo-search requests beautifulsoup4 lxml
```

**Estimated cost per company:** ~$0.08–$0.15 (Claude Opus 4.7 with adaptive thinking)

---

## Step 3 — Batch Process a CSV

```bash
# Process all companies in leads.csv, skip score < 7
python pillar-a/outreach/scripts/batch_pipeline.py \
  --input pillar-a/outreach/leads.csv \
  --output pillar-a/outreach/output/ \
  --min-score 7

# Runs each company through research_lead.py
# Writes summary table to output/batch_summary.md
# Estimated time: ~2 min per company
```

---

## Step 4 — Review Research Output

Open each `output/<company>_research.json` and check:

- [ ] `funding_stage` matches what Gemini found
- [ ] `aws_signals` are real (not guessed)
- [ ] `estimated_aws_spend` range is reasonable (not $500k/mo for a 30-person startup)
- [ ] `contacts[0].name` is a real person (Google them quickly)
- [ ] `FinOps_tools_detected` — if empty, that's a green flag
- [ ] `personalization_hooks` — pick the strongest one

**Fix anything that looks wrong before moving to Step 5.**

---

## Step 5 — Review & Edit the Email

Open `output/<company>_emails.md`:

1. Pick the best variant (usually Email 1 or 2)
2. Edit the first sentence — make it more specific and human
3. Fill in `[Your name]` and your Calendly link
4. Check word count — target: 60–90 words

---

## Step 6 — Send

**Option A (manual):** Copy email body + subject into Gmail. BCC yourself.

**Option B (Instantly.ai):**
1. Import the contact (name, email, company) into Instantly
2. Create a campaign using EMAIL_SEQUENCES.md templates
3. Paste your customised email as Day 1, keep Day 4 and Day 8 as-is

**Option C (Lemlist):** Same as Instantly but with better LinkedIn integration.

---

## Step 7 — Log in CRM

Open your Notion CRM (see `docs/CRM_TRACKER.md`):

- Add a row: Company, Contact, Email, Stage = "Contacted", Next Action = "Follow up Day 4"
- Set Next Action Date = today + 4 days
- Paste the sent email in Notes

---

## Accuracy / Hallucination Notes

The AI is useful for speed. It is not always right. Rules:

1. **Never send an email that references something you haven't verified.** If Claude says "I saw your blog post on AWS migration" — Google it. Confirm it exists.
2. **Never guess an email address without verification.** Use hunter.io, apollo.io, or LinkedIn.
3. **AWS spend estimates are educated guesses** — don't put them in the email. Use as internal qualification signal only.
4. **ICP score is a guide, not a gate.** A score of 6 with a strong personalization hook is better than a score of 10 with nothing personal to say.
