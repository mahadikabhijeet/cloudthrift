The process

Read-only. Automated.
Done in 48 hours.

No agents to install, no write access — ever. Our audit engine does the legwork so your engineers stay focused on shipping product.

Step — 5 minutes01

Deploy a read-only role

You launch our CloudFormation template, which creates a read-only IAM role in your account — locked to us with a unique ExternalId. No write permissions exist, so nothing in your infrastructure can be changed.

Step — automated02

We scan everything

Our audit engine runs 20+ automated checks across every AWS region: zombie resources, idle compute, over-provisioned databases, network waste, and Savings Plans & Reserved Instance coverage gaps.

Step — within 48h03

You get the findings

An interactive dashboard, a PDF report, and a 30-minute walkthrough call — every finding sized in dollars per month, ranked by effort, with exact remediation steps.

Zero risk: remove the role with one command at any time. Most teams uncover 20–40% in monthly savings.
